Delete your Race Ya! account

You can permanently delete your Race Ya! account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

Open Race Ya! → Settings → Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your account, activities, routes, photos, race records, wagers, progression, and profile from our servers, and wipes the app's data on your device.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email raceyagame@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account: authentication record, profile, recorded activities and GPS routes, attached photos, race records, wager history, follows, challenges, and progression (XP, titles, loot state). Data other users hold about their own races is theirs and is not affected.

Partial deletion

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove data: individual activities (including their routes and photos) can be deleted inside the app at any time and are removed from our servers on the next sync.
