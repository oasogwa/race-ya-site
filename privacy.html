Race Ya! Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Race Ya! ("the app") is a GPS activity tracking and racing app developed by Onyebuchi Asogwa ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The short version: your data exists to make your own tracking and racing work, we do not sell it, and the app shows no ads.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials, handled by our backend provider Supabase. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.

Location and activity data. The core of the app. While you record a run, walk, or ride, the app collects precise GPS location to build your route, distance, and pace. Recording continues while your screen is off through an Android foreground service with a visible notification, and only while a recording you started is in progress. The app never tracks your location in the background outside of an active recording. If you connect a heart rate sensor, heart rate readings are recorded with the activity.

Fitness and social content. Activities you save, photos you attach to them, your profile display name and customizations, the friends you follow, race results, group challenges, and friend wagers (including their outcomes and integrity flags) are stored so they can sync across your devices and be shared as described below.

Purchases. If you subscribe to Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play. Our subscription provider RevenueCat receives a purchase token and an anonymous app user ID to validate your entitlement. We never see your payment card details.

Notifications. A device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) is stored so we can deliver wager and social notifications you have enabled.

What we do not collect. No advertising identifiers, no contact lists, no data from other apps, and no location outside an active recording.

How your information is used

To record and display your activities; to sync your data across your devices; to run the social features you use (following, racing friends' paces, wagers, challenges); to validate Pro subscriptions; to send notifications you have enabled; and to maintain the integrity of races and wagers. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Data is shared only as the features require. People you allow to follow you can see the activities you share, including routes, and wager participants see wager submissions. Our service providers process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Google Firebase (push notifications), Mapbox (map display, routing, and road matching; map requests include your device IP and the coordinates being displayed or matched), RevenueCat (subscription validation), and Google Play (billing). If you choose to connect Strava, data is exchanged with Strava under their terms only after you authorize it.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete individual activities in the app at any time, which removes them from our servers on the next sync. To delete your account and all associated data, use the delete option in the app's settings, or email us at the address below from your account email and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between the app and our servers, and our backend enforces row-level security so your data is only readable by you and the people your sharing choices allow.

Children

Race Ya! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
